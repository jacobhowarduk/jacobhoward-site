I am an economist working on wealth distribution and long-run wealth dynamics. I am completing an MSc in Economics at the University of Greenwich.

My dissertation asks what the constant in the standard return–growth specification actually is. Under both of the competing frameworks it turns out not to be a free parameter, which changes what the specification can be used to test. More on the research.

Before the MSc I read Economics at Greenwich, where I helped build the Sunday Times Rich List panel with Benjamin Tippet and wrote a dissertation on the returns of the UK wealth elite.
